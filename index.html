The path forward

From ambition
to exam day.

A clear process to help prepared students take their next step.

01

Apply & verify

Tell us about your exam and goals. We'll follow up about identity and testing-profile verification.

02

Take a practice test

We'll send practice-test instructions and review your score. The benchmark is typically 70–80%, depending on the exam.

03

Arrange your exam

If approved, we'll contact you to arrange your exam using an exam voucher. No cash is transferred to applicants.

04

Report & pay it forward

Share your official score report after the exam. An alumni pledge to help future students is optional.

Clear expectations

Trust is part of the process.

These guidelines help us support students and make each grant count.

No cash transfers

Applicants do not receive cash. Approved exam support is arranged through the fund.

48-hour notice

If your schedule changes, tell us at least 48 hours in advance. Your testing provider may have additional rules.

Conditional grant agreement

If awarded a grant, you agree to give 48-hour notice for schedule changes and provide your official score report within 14 days after your exam, regardless of the result.

The details

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does the fund help cover?

The fund is intended to cover eligible professional IT and technology certification exam fees. Applications are subject to review and available funding.

What happens after I apply?

The intended process includes a follow-up about verification and a practice test. This website is currently in test mode; use sample information only.

What if I need to change my exam date?

Give at least 48 hours' notice and follow any additional requirements from your testing provider.

Do I need to pass the exam to report my score?

No. Under the displayed grant agreement, an official score report is requested within 14 days regardless of the result.

Is the website accepting real applications?

No. The application and admin dashboard are in test mode. Sample submissions are stored in the shared database.
