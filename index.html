The path forward

From ambition
to certified.

How a grant works, from application to score report.

Step 1: Apply & verify

Tell us about the certification or credential you want and why. We'll follow up about identity and, where relevant, testing-profile verification.

Identity verification

Step 2: Take a practice test

For exam-based credentials, we'll send practice-test instructions and review your score. The benchmark is typically 70 to 80%, depending on the exam.

70 to 80% benchmark

Step 3: Get it covered

If approved, we'll contact you to arrange payment directly, such as an exam voucher or enrollment with the provider. No cash is transferred to applicants.

No cash transfers

Step 4: Report & pay it forward

Share your official score report or certificate once you finish. An alumni pledge to help future students is optional.

Optional pledge

Clear expectations

What every grant requires.

Every grant follows the same rules.

No cash transfers

Applicants do not receive cash. Approved support is arranged through the fund.

48-hour notice

If your schedule changes, tell us at least 48 hours in advance. Your testing provider may have additional rules.

Conditional grant agreement

If awarded a grant, you agree to give 48-hour notice for schedule changes and provide your official score report or certificate within 14 days after your exam or completion, regardless of the result.

The details

Frequently asked
questions.

What does the fund help cover?

The fund is intended to cover eligible certification and credentialing fees that students would otherwise pay for themselves, such as industry exams or platform certificates like Bloomberg Market Concepts and Coursera. Applications are subject to review and available funding.

What happens after I apply?

The intended process includes a follow-up about verification and a practice test. This website is currently in test mode; use sample information only.

What if I need to change my exam or start date?

Give at least 48 hours' notice and follow any additional requirements from your testing provider.

Do I need to pass to report my results?

No. The grant agreement asks for your official score report or certificate within 14 days, whatever the result.

Is the website accepting real applications?

No. The application and admin dashboard are in test mode. Sample submissions are stored in the shared database.
