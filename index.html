A clear path forward

Four steps to exam day.

A straightforward process designed to support prepared students and keep every grant accountable.

01

Apply & verify

Tell us about your exam and verify your identity and testing profile.

02

Show you're ready

Share a practice test score. The readiness benchmark is typically 70–80%, depending on the exam.

03

Book securely

If approved, receive a direct voucher or join a live co-booking session. No cash changes hands.

04

Report & pay it forward

Share your official score report after the exam. If you'd like, make an optional alumni pledge to help future students.

The fine print, made simple

Trust is part of the process.

Clear expectations protect applicants and help the fund support more students.

✓ Zero cash transfers

Grant funds go directly toward an exam voucher or booking with the provider. We don't send cash to applicants.

↻ Need to reschedule?

Give at least 48 hours' notice for schedule changes, and follow your testing provider's own cancellation and rescheduling rules.

♡ A grant with shared commitments

If you're awarded a grant, you agree to give 48 hours' notice for schedule changes and submit your official score report within 14 days of your exam. Passing is the goal, but your report is requested regardless of the result. An alumni pledge is always optional.
