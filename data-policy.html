Data governance

Data Handling and Retention Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The website is currently in test mode. This policy describes the intended launch process and must be reviewed with Hack Club Bank before real applicant information is collected.

1. Purpose

This policy establishes how The LevelUp Fund collects, accesses, uses, protects, retains, exports, and deletes grant-application and support data. It supports the commitments in our Privacy Policy.

2. Data-minimization rules

  • Collect only fields shown in the approved application and support forms.
  • Do not request Social Security numbers, passwords, bank details, government ID files, medical records, or full dates of birth through the website.
  • Do not place applicant information in public documents, GitHub issues, source code, screenshots, or unapproved spreadsheets.
  • Use sample information while the website displays Test mode.

3. Access and accountability

  • Each administrator must use an individual Supabase account. Shared passwords are prohibited.
  • Only people listed in the administrator allowlist may access applications or support requests.
  • Access must be removed promptly when an administrator leaves the team or no longer needs it.
  • Administrators may use applicant information only for fund operations, support, security, auditing, or required legal compliance.
  • Administrators must sign out on shared devices and must not download records to personal devices unless an approved operational need exists.

Legal holds, active disputes, fraud investigations, sponsor requirements, or applicable law may require a longer period. Hack Club Bank's approved retention requirements control where they differ from this draft.

5. Deletion process

  • An authorized administrator reviews records at least quarterly for expiration.
  • Expired records are deleted through the admin dashboard or an approved database procedure.
  • Verified privacy requests are completed within a reasonable time unless retention is required.
  • Deletion removes the active database record. Encrypted provider backups may remain until they rotate out under the provider's backup schedule.
  • The administrator records completion without copying the deleted applicant's sensitive answers into a separate log.

6. Security controls

  • HTTPS for all public and administrative traffic.
  • Turnstile, honeypots, server-side validation, idempotency, and hashed-identifier rate limits for public submissions.
  • Row-level database security, direct-insert blocking, least-privilege column grants, and individual admin authentication.
  • Private keys stored only as hosted secrets, never in browser code or GitHub.
  • Dependency and security-boundary tests run before deployment.

7. External services

Approved service providers may process data only for their operational purpose. Current website services include Supabase, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Turnstile, and an administrator's chosen email provider. Administrators must not paste essays or other applicant information into AI or analysis services unless Hack Club Bank has approved that workflow and the applicant has received appropriate notice or provided permission.

8. Incident response

  • Immediately stop or restrict affected access without weakening database protections.
  • Preserve relevant security logs without broadly copying applicant data.
  • Notify the designated fund lead and Hack Club Bank promptly.
  • Determine affected records, people, dates, and systems.
  • Follow sponsor and legal notification requirements.
  • Document remediation and verify protections before restoring normal service.

9. Requests and corrections

People may request access, correction, or deletion through the support form using “Privacy or data request.” Administrators must verify the requester's identity before disclosing or changing records.

10. Review

The fund will review this policy at least annually and whenever the application fields, service providers, eligibility process, or sponsor requirements materially change.
