Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The website is currently in test mode. Use made-up information only. This policy must be reviewed with Hack Club Bank before real applications are accepted.

1. Who we are

The LevelUp Fund is a student-led microfund intended to help eligible students pay certification and credential fees. This policy explains how the fund handles information submitted through its website and admin tools.

2. Information we collect

Grant applications

  • Full legal name and email address.
  • Confirmation that the applicant is at least 14 years old. We do not request a date of birth.
  • Certification name, administrator, website, and planned test date.
  • The requested grant amount and reported annual household income.
  • The applicant's essay and agreement to the conditional grant terms.
  • Application status, practice score, and administrator notes created during review.

Support requests

  • Name, email, category, subject, and message.
  • Confirmation that the person contacting us is at least 14 or is a parent or guardian.
  • Support status and internal administrator notes.

Security and technical information

Cloudflare Turnstile and our submission services may process technical information needed to prevent abuse, such as IP address and browser signals. The fund stores only keyed hashes of IP and email identifiers in its short-lived rate-limit records, not the raw identifiers in that rate-limit table.

3. Why we use information

  • Review eligibility and administer grants.
  • Contact applicants about verification, practice testing, decisions, scheduling, and results.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Protect the website, enforce rate limits, prevent fraud, and investigate security problems.
  • Maintain records required for nonprofit, financial, audit, or legal obligations.
  • Improve the application and support process using aggregated or de-identified information.

4. Who can access information

Application and support records are limited to individually authorized LevelUp Fund administrators. We may share information with Hack Club Bank or other service providers only when reasonably necessary to review, administer, pay, audit, secure, or comply with legal requirements related to a grant.

Providers used by the website may include Supabase for authentication, database storage, and server functions; GitHub Pages for website hosting; Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection; and an administrator's email provider when an administrator chooses to prepare or send a reply.

5. AI-writing checks

The website does not automatically send essays to an AI-detection service. An administrator may manually copy an essay into an external service only when appropriate permission and an approved process exist. AI-detection results can be wrong and must never be the sole basis for a grant decision.

6. Selling and advertising

We do not sell applicant or support information. We do not use it for behavioral advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain records only for the periods described in our Data Handling and Retention Policy, subject to nonprofit, accounting, dispute, security, and legal requirements.

8. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with you. Some information may need to be retained for grant administration, financial records, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or an active dispute. Submit a support request and choose “Privacy or data request.” We may need to verify your identity before acting.

9. Applicants and children

Applicants must be at least 14 years old. The support form is for people who are at least 14 or for a parent or guardian contacting us. We do not knowingly request applications from children under 14. If you believe a child submitted information contrary to this policy, use the support form so we can investigate and delete it when appropriate.

10. Security

We use HTTPS, individual administrator accounts, access controls, row-level database security, bot checks, rate limits, validation, and restricted server-side submission paths. No system is perfectly secure, so do not submit passwords, government identification numbers, bank information, or other information the forms do not request.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the program, providers, or legal requirements change. We will change the date above and publish material changes before they apply.

12. Contact

Submit privacy and support questions through the LevelUp Fund support form.
